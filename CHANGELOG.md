# Changelog

All notable changes to this template are documented here.
The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [1.0.0] — Initial release

### Added

- Home page with all 17 sections: hero, motto, what's in our community,
  platform highlights, core features, numbers, powerful features, mobile app,
  download app, pricing, comparison, about us, who can use, team, process, FAQ,
  final CTA and testimonials.
- Pricing, Contact, Waitlist, Changelog, Privacy Policy pages and a custom 404.
- Fixed pill header with an "All Pages" menu, plus a hamburger menu for tablet
  and mobile.
- Hero entrance choreography reproduced from the original timings — staggered
  springs for the badge, headline, copy, button and visual, and eased tweens for
  the light rays and floating widget columns.
- Continuous marquees: the trusted-by logo row, three platform-highlight ticker
  rows, the app-screen ticker, the About testimonial strip and three vertical
  testimonial columns.
- Contact and waitlist forms with client-side validation and loading, success
  and error states, behind a single swappable transport.
- Design tokens for colour, radii, typography and motion; the project's three
  real breakpoints (809 / 810–1319 / 1320+).
- SEO: per-page metadata, Open Graph and Twitter cards, canonical URLs,
  `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` and Organization JSON-LD.
- Accessibility: skip link, semantic landmarks, keyboard-operable menus and
  accordions, labelled form fields, live status regions and reduced-motion
  support.

### Notes

- The blog is deliberately absent: no routes, components, assets or links.
